Atom Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 2, 2026

Atom records Google Meet sessions only after a signed-in user submits a meeting link and grants Google Drive upload permission.

Information We Use

Atom uses your Google account email and Google account identifier to sign you in, associate sessions with your account, upload completed recordings to your Google Drive, and send delivery email to your Gmail address.

Google Drive Access

Atom requests the limited Google Drive file permission needed to create recording files in your Drive. Atom does not request broad access to read or manage all files in your Drive.

Recordings

Atom temporarily processes meeting audio and video so it can create the recording file. After delivery to your Google Drive and email, Atom deletes the temporary server copy. Atom keeps delivery history such as meeting title, duration, delivery status, and summary text.

Gmail Delivery

Atom emails the finished recording link to the signed-in user's email address. Atom does not read your Gmail inbox.

Sharing

Atom does not sell user data. Data is used only to provide sign-in, meeting recording, Drive upload, email delivery, troubleshooting, and security.

Data Removal

You can delete delivery history from your Atom profile. You can remove Atom's access from your Google Account permissions page at any time.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner through the support email listed on Atom's Google OAuth consent screen.
